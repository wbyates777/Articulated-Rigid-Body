by W.B. Yates

Polytopes are the generalization of three-dimensional polyhedra to any number of dimensions. The GJK algorithm works directly on their convex-hull. However the convex-hull is never computed explicitly, instead each GJK-iteration employs a support function that has a cost linearly dependent on the number of points defining the polytope.
