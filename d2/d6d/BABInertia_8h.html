Detailed Description

A compact representation of spatial articulated body inertia (see Featherstone, RBDA, page 245, 247). 

  $$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$
  $   BABInertia.h   $
  $$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$

date 09/08/2025

by W.B. Yates

Articulated body inertia is a generalisation of spatial inertia and is defined as a 6x6 matrix 

    | I_o  H |
    | H^T  M |

where 

    I_o is the rotational inertia at body frame origin,
    H is the generalised inertia matrix, initially H = arb::cross(h), 
    M is generalised mass, initially M = m * B_IDENTITY_3x3,

and 

    m is mass,
    com is centre of mass, 
    h = m * com is linear momentum.

ABInertia is only used (at the moment) inside BDynamics::forward() for holding interim 'articulated inertias' that are linked together in a kinematic chain/tree of bodies. In order to represent the (spatial) inertia of a single body use BRBInertia
