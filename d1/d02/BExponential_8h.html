Detailed Description

Spatial exponential and logarithmic maps for motion vectors. 

  $$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$
  $   BExponential.h   $
  $$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$$

date 04/04/2026

by W.B. Yates

Given an arbitrary twist, the spatial transform $X = exp(v)$ represents the integrated displacement over a unit time step Δt.

If $v$ is expressed in the base/world coordinate frame we have:

X(t + Δt) = X(t) exp(vΔt)

or if $v$ is expressed in body frame coordinates.

X(t + Δt) = exp(vΔt) X(t)

The log mapping is the inverse of exp. In symbols:

log( exp(v) ) = v

Lie group terminology:

  • transforms $X$ are elements of a Lie group,
  • velocity vectors $v$ are elements of a Lie algebra,
  • exp:LieAlg --> LieGrp,
  • SE(3) - Lie group of rigid body transforms,
  • se(3) - tangent space at identity (Lie Algebra).
Note
we use the Featherstone convention on transforms, i.e X(E,r) as opposed to the homogenous convention X = (R,p), where E = R^T and r = -R^Tp
arb::cross(w) * arb::cross(w) == arb::outer(w,w) - (arb::dot(w,w) * B_IDENTITY_3x3);
SO(3) is the Lie group of Matrix3 rotations, while so(3) is the associated tangent space.
