15 @brief A wrapper for the specific GJK implementation used to detect collisions.
16
17 Narrow-phase collision detection.
18 My c++ wrapper for external c library libcdd.
19
20 An implemetation of:
21
22 1) the Gilbert–Johnson–Keerthi (GJK) distance algorithm, and
23 2) the Expanding-Polytope-Algorithm (EPA).
24
25 Computes penetration of body2 into body1. Returns distance/depth of penetration, contact normal direction,
26 and contact position.
27
28 It is not the most recent implementation of GJK but it is well tested, fairly efficient and robust.
29 Also it does not depend directly on Eigen.
30
31 @see https://github.com/danfis/libccd
32 @see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert–Johnson–Keerthi_distance_algorithm
